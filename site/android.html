How to install Silsigan on Android

Closed testing — join the tester group first, then install from Play.

  1. Open the tester group: https://groups.google.com/g/silsigan-testers
  2. Log in to Google with the same email as your Play Store account.
  3. Click Join group.
  4. Open the testing page: https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.silsigan.app
  5. Click Join as tester.
  6. After you join, tap the blue Download it on Google Play link on that page, or go here: Download Silsigan on Google Play
